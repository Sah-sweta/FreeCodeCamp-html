Rise Beyond Movie Review

Rise Beyond is a 2022 short drama film that follows the emotional journey of Ethan Carter, a passionate young man from a small town who faces severe personal and financial hardships while pursuing his lifelong dream of making music. Supported by a guiding mentor and a loyal friend, the story focuses on resilience, overcoming intense self-doubt, and finding the strength to move past life's obstacles.
