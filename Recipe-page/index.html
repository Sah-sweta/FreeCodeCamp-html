Halwa Recipe

Recipe

Ingredients

  • suji
  • Ghee
  • Sugar
  • Dry fruits
  • water

Instructions

  1. Add some ghee to the heated pan and roast dry friuts and keep it aside
  2. In the same pan with some ghee pour 1 cup of suji and roast until its golden.
  3. Add 3 cups of warm water to the roasted suji. Keep stirring until is water is absorbed by suji.
  4. Add sugar and mix them well.
  5. Add some ghee cook for 2-3 minutes. Add roasted dryfruits and its ready to serve
